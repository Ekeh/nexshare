**NEXSHARE**

*Product & Business Specification — Universal Performance-Commerce Network*

Version 1.0 | August 2026

# 1. Executive Summary

NexShare is a universal performance-commerce and performance-marketing network. Businesses publish measurable commercial offers; approved promoters distribute those offers through their own audiences and channels; customers take qualifying actions; businesses pay according to campaign terms; and NexShare handles tracking, attribution, commissions, settlement and analytics.

NexShare is industry-agnostic. An offer may represent a physical product, digital product, service, booking, subscription, lead, appointment, application, event, property opportunity, or another legitimate measurable commercial action.

Promoters are paid for qualifying results, not merely for posting. The long-term vision is a distributed digital sales force that allows people to generate income without owning inventory.

# 2. Vision

Build a large African digital customer-acquisition network in which ordinary people, students, graduates, creators, influencers and professional promoters can earn income by connecting businesses with customers.

# 3. The Core Model

| Participant | Role | Economic result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business | Publishes offer, campaign, approved brand assets and conversion rules | Gets measurable customers/results |
| Promoter | Selects offers, distributes content and generates conversions | Earns commission on qualifying results |
| Customer | Purchases or completes the qualifying action | Receives product/service/offer |
| NexShare | Provides technology, attribution, analytics, fraud controls and settlement | Earns platform share/fees |

# 6. Promoter Experience

1. Register and verify account.
2. Browse available offers and campaigns.
3. Review commission and conversion requirements.
4. Select an offer.
5. Generate a unique tracking link, QR code or approved creative.
6. Share through permitted channels.
7. Customer interacts and completes the qualifying action.
8. System attributes and validates the result.
9. Commission moves from pending to approved/available.
10. Promoter withdraws eligible earnings through supported payout methods.

# 9. Business-Funded Campaigns

Businesses can create campaigns that explicitly recruit promoters. A campaign may specify commission, total budget, maximum conversions, dates, geography, promoter eligibility, approved assets and conversion requirements. Campaigns automatically stop when configured limits are reached.

# 10. Commission Models

| Model | Example | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Percentage | 5% of qualifying sale | Product/service sales |
| Fixed | K50 per sale | Simple transactions |
| Qualified lead | K100 per verified lead | Education, insurance, real estate, B2B |
| Booking | K75 per completed booking | Hotels, travel, restaurants |
| Milestone | K500 after verified milestone | High-value services/property |
| Tiered | K50, then K75, then K100 | Campaign incentives |
| Bonus | Temporary extra reward | Launch campaigns |

# 11. Commission Lifecycle

A typical commission lifecycle is: Clicked → Attributed → Conversion created → Validation → Approved → Available → Paid. Product sales may require delivery and return-window expiry; leads may require business verification; bookings may require completed service. Rules are campaign-specific.

# 12. Branded External Promotion

When a promoter shares an offer externally, the social preview and approved creative should primarily display the participating company's name, logo, imagery, offer, price and call to action. NexShare is the underlying tracking and performance infrastructure. Required affiliate, advertising and consumer-protection disclosures must remain available where applicable.

- Business-approved logo
- Business-approved imagery
- Business-approved claims
- Offer title and price
- Business call to action
- Unique promoter attribution
- Required disclosure

# 14. Student & Graduate Opportunity

NexShare can provide a dedicated student/graduate program focused on digital commerce skills and income generation. Marketing must not promise guaranteed employment or guaranteed income; earnings depend on qualifying results.

# 15. Customer Referral Layer

Customers may also receive referral links or shareable offers and earn rewards or commissions where a campaign permits it. Consumer rewards should be governed by explicit rules and should not require recruitment to generate earnings.

# 16. Growth Flywheel

More businesses → more offers → more promoter opportunities → more distribution → more customers → more conversions → more business results → more promoter earnings → more participation → more businesses.
